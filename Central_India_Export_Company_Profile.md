## 1. Executive Summary & Corporate Profile

**Central India Export (CIE)** is a premier Indian manufacturer and international B2B exporter specializing in certified, micron-precision ophthalmic surgical Consumables and Instruments, IOVUE™ intraocular lenses (IOLs), micro-surgical blades, ophthalmic solutions, and other Ophthalmic Consumables and equipments. 

Founded in **2004** in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India by Founder & CEO **Sandeep Vaid**, Central India Export has grown over 21 years into a globally recognized brand and trusted partner for hospital networks, surgical clinics, government health tenders, and medical device distributors worldwide.

Headquartered at Nagpur's Zero Mile in the heart of the city, CIE operates 3 owned cleanroom production facilities and serves over **31 countries with a strict policy of ONE COUNTRY ONE DISTRIBUTOR NETWORK**.

## 4. Manufacturing Infrastructure: 4 Dedicated Cleanroom Production Units

Central India Export operates **3 owned manufacturing units in Nagpur** alongside strategic partner production lines, manufacturing over **400 catalog items**:

### Unit 01: Cleanroom IOL Assembly & CNC Lathe Line (MIHAN Hub)
* **Infrastructure:** ISO Class 5 and Class 7 certified cleanroom environments under laminar air flow.
* **Machinery:** CNC optical lathes for 360° Square Edge IOL optic milling, haptic attachment, and 100% hydration diopter power testing.
* **Capabilities:** Hydrophilic, Hydrophobic, and PMMA optic lathe cutting with interferometric surface inspection.

### Unit 02: Micro Surgical Blade Laser Honing Line (Ramdaspeth Facility)
* **Infrastructure:** Precision grinding shop with Class 5 clean bench assembly.
* **Machinery:** Laser-guided edge honing equipment and Swiss micro-grinding machinery.
* **Capabilities:** Production of 1.8mm to 3.2mm Keratome, Lance, Slit, Crescent, and MVR sideport blades, each inspected under **100x digital magnification**.

### Unit 03: Sterile Solutions BFS Liquid Filling Line (Packaging Facility)
* **Infrastructure:** Aseptic pharmaceutical liquid filling cleanroom.
* **Machinery:** Automated Blow-Fill-Seal (BFS) liquid aseptic filling systems and industrial Ethylene Oxide (ETO) gas sterilizers.
* **Capabilities:** 1.0%–1.4% Sodium Hyaluronate viscoelastics filled in pre-filled sterile syringes with tamper-proof blister sealing.

### Unit 04: Prosthetics & Diagnostic Strip Laboratory
* **Infrastructure:** Optical finishing and paper processing lab.
* **Machinery:** High-speed precision acrylic polishing stations and precision paper slitting machinery.
* **Capabilities:** Hand-polished PMMA ocular artificial eyes in custom iris shades and lint-free medical paper diagnostic strips (Fluorescein, Schirmer).
